Evidence Boundary

Attendance/OJT Hours measure time and completion. Daily Performance contains manual day ratings. Work contains task scores and Work-based analytics. Logbook contains submitted evidence and review state. Official Evaluation is the separate formal supervisor-entered evaluation. No combined OJT grade is calculated here.

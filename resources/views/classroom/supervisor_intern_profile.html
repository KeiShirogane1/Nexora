Separate Performance Dimensions

Attendance and OJT Hours track time and completion. Daily Performance records manual day ratings. Work tracks task scoring and Work-based analytics. Logbook records evidence and review status. Official Evaluation is the separate formal supervisor-entered evaluation. Nexora does not combine these five dimensions into a sixth overall OJT grade.
